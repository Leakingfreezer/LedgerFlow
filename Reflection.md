**Reflection**
Originally, this was an MSE 121 project where we were assigned to create a financial budgeting tool on Excel VBA code and pivot tables.
In less than a week, I created a basic budgeting tool that was targetted towards students, specifically noticing the problem that univeristy students
have very little stable income and rely on co-op salaries, allowances, OSAP, etc. This very reason makes it more cruical for them to understand and manage their expenses
however, most budgeting apps are not considerate of these circumstances and also have lots of manual entry. I wanted to create a behaviour finance tool that made it quick and 
easy for users to input their monthly or even daily expenses, visualize where their money is going and how and ultimately learn more about their spending habits.
